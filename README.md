### 💌 A Lil About Me

I study Software Engineering at Babcock University, but most of my real learning happens at 2am building random ideas nobody asked me to build. I build web + mobile apps from scratch, run my own design brand, and I'm low-key obsessed with turning side projects into things that actually make money one day 🌸

not a "10x engineer," just someone who really likes finishing what she starts (and then immediately starting three more things).

---

### 🌷 What I'm About RN

- 🛠️ Building small, scrappy products and seeing if real people would actually use them
- 💸 Learning how to take a side project from "cute idea" to "this makes money"
- 🎨 Keeping one foot in design so my code doesn't just work, it looks good too
- 📈 Figuring out distribution — building it is the easy part, getting people to care is the real project
